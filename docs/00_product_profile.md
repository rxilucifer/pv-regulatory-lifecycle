## 2. Fictional development programme

Three phase 3 trials gave DMX-101 to 2,900 participants in total. All numbers are invented. The adverse reactions below were the ones seen in these trials, and they are the reference safety information used in this project until a label is written in a later module.

| Adverse reaction | Frequency category |
|---|---|
| Nausea | Common |
| Headache | Common |
| Dizziness | Uncommon |

Frequency categories used in this project:

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Very common | 1 in 10 or more |
| Common | 1 in 100 to fewer than 1 in 10 |
| Uncommon | 1 in 1,000 to fewer than 1 in 100 |
| Rare | 1 in 10,000 to fewer than 1 in 1,000 |
| Very rare | Fewer than 1 in 10,000 |

## 3. How this product is used in the project

- Module 1 invents safety reports for DMX-101, with planted problems and an answer key.
- Later modules process the reports, find a possible signal, evaluate it, update the label and write the related documents.
- Every document in this project follows the [style guide](01_style_guide.md) and carries the control table above.

## 4. Open decisions

- Which event will become the possible signal (decided in Module 1).
- The reporting period used for the periodic safety report (decided in Module 5).
