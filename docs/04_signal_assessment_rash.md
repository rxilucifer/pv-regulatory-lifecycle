## Purpose
Records the screening result for rash in the fictional DMX-101 safety database.

## Data used
486 reports after duplicates were removed and rows with a missing or impossible age were set aside (see PV-004).

## Method
For each event, the proportional reporting ratio (PRR) and chi-square were calculated for a group against all other reports. Groups: 25 mg, age 65 and over, and 25 mg with age 65 and over. An event met the screening rule with at least 3 reports, PRR of 2 or more and chi-square of 4 or more.

## Result
Rash is not listed in the reference safety information (PV-001). It was the most frequently reported unlisted event, with 58 reports. It was the only event that met the screening rule.

| Group | Rash reports | PRR | Chi-square |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 mg | 50 | 5.95 | 32.2 |
| Age 65 and over | 37 | 3.73 | 30.4 |
| 25 mg and age 65 and over | 32 | 6.96 | 83.2 |

## Assessment
Rash was reported more often than expected, mainly at 25 mg and in patients aged 65 and over. This is a statistical screening result and does not show that DMX-101 causes rash.

## Limits
- All data is invented.
- The comparison is within one database, not against other drugs.
- Individual cases have not yet been assessed for causality.
- Reports may overlap between groups, so the three rows are not independent.

## Proposed next steps
- Review the individual rash cases using the WHO-UMC scale (PV-002).
- Check the 25 mg and age 65 and over group for other explanations such as other medicines.
- Decide whether the signal should be validated and how it would be communicated (Modules 4 to 8).
