**DMX-101 (fictional): rash reported, mainly at 25 mg and in patients aged 65 and over**

Dear Healthcare Professional,

## Summary
- Rash has been reported in patients taking DMX-101, mainly at the 25 mg dose and in patients aged 65 years and over.
- Please tell patients to contact their doctor if a rash appears.
- Please consider rash when reviewing patients aged 65 and over who take the 25 mg dose.
- The product information is being updated.

## Background on the safety concern
DMX-101 is a once-daily tablet used as an add-on treatment for type 2 diabetes. Rash is not in the current product information.

By 30 June 2026, 61 reports of rash had been received among 483 reports in total, 18 of them serious. Serious includes reports with a fatal outcome (PV-007). Most rash reports were at 25 mg and in patients aged 65 and over (PV-005). These are reports of events and do not show that DMX-101 is the cause. The frequency cannot be estimated because no patient exposure data is available.

## Follow-up
Example Pharma Ltd will review the individual cases and will report the result in the next periodic safety update report.

## Call for reporting
Please report suspected adverse reactions to Example Pharma Ltd or through the national pharmacovigilance programme.
